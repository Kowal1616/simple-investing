Privacy Policy

ZenETFs respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or process personal data.

No Personal Data Collection

ZenETFs is a read-only educational tool. We do not offer user accounts, registration forms, newsletters, or any mechanism that would collect personal information. You can use the entire website without providing any data about yourself.

Analytics

We use Umami Analytics — a privacy-first, GDPR-compliant analytics tool. It collects only anonymous, aggregated data such as:

  • Page views (which pages are visited)
  • Country and browser type (aggregated, no IP storage)
  • Device type (desktop/mobile)

No cookies are set. No personal identifiers are collected. No data is shared with third parties.

External Links

The website contains links to external sites (e.g. justETF.com for fund details). Once you leave ZenETFs, the privacy policy of the destination website applies. We are not responsible for the practices of third-party sites.
